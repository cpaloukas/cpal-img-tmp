## LinkedIn (English)
Thank you to Paypercut for supporting WordCamp Athens 2026 as a Premium sponsor.

Paypercut is a European payment platform that gives online stores one checkout for payments across Europe. It accepts cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Buy Now Pay Later, in 11 currencies and more than 20 countries, and pays out to the merchant's existing bank account. It has ready-made extensions for WooCommerce, Shopify, PrestaShop, Magento and OpenCart, and most merchants go live within 48 hours.

From next Friday, December 11, the Greek WordPress community spends two days at the War Museum. Get your ticket now:
https://athens.wordcamp.org/2026/tickets/
